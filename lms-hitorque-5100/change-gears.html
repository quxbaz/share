Gear positions

  • A is the first (top) change gear. It is driven from the spindle through internal headstock gears, not mounted on the spindle.
  • B (inside) and C (outside) share the middle shaft. A drives B, and C drives D.
  • D sits on the end of the lead screw.
  • any: three-gear setup. Any gear in B meshes A with D without changing the ratio, and any gear in C is only a spacer. Move the D spacer outside the gear so D lines up with B.

Ratios

Four gears: TPI = 16 × (B × D) / (A × C)
Three gears: TPI = 16 × D / A
Feed per rev = 1 / TPI. Pitch (mm) = 25.4 / TPI, so metric pitches are approximate. Actual is what each setup cuts, and Deviance is how far that is from the nominal pitch.
